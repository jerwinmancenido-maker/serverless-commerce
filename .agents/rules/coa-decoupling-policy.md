# Strict COA Decoupling & Independent Page Policy

## Non-Negotiable Directives

1. **NO COA CONNECTION ON ANY PRODUCT PAGE**:
   - Never attach, mount, link, or display Certificates of Analysis (COA) on individual product pages (`/products/[handle]`) or product tabs (`product-tabs/index.tsx`).
   - Product pages must focus solely on analytical chemistry specifications, purity specifications (e.g., ≥99.0%), and standard formulation options without inline COA integrations.

2. **ABSOLUTELY NO FAKE OR DUMMY COA DOCUMENTS**:
   - Never generate simulated, placeholder, fallback, or synthetic COA documents, chromatograms, or mock modals.
   - Any COA documentation must come from authentic, verified analytical laboratory records.

3. **COA MUST BE TREATED AS AN INDEPENDENT PAGE**:
   - Certificates of Analysis exist exclusively as a separate, independent directory/page (such as `/research-library#coa`).
   - Do NOT edit, touch, generate, or alter COA systems or documents without Jerwin's explicit directive and say-so.
